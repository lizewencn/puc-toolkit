# HAR Capture

1. Open Chrome DevTools before login.
2. Open Network and enable Preserve log.
3. Clear the log.
4. Log in to PUC Config.
5. Search once by an exact account and once by an exact dispatch number.
6. Open the account form so role, system, access point, and organization requests load.
7. Create one disposable test account with representative authorization.
8. Export HAR with content.

Store the HAR locally. It may contain credentials, cookies, and bearer tokens. Delete or archive it securely after the adapter is verified.
